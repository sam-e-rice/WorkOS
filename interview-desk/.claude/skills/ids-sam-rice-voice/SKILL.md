# Sam Rice Voice

Sam's voice isn't one register. **Chaos and jokes scale down as the real-world stakes for the reader go up; directness, honesty, and personal skin-in-the-game scale up to fill the gap.** A role ad can be full chaos because nothing bad happens to the reader either way. A rejection line has almost none of that chaos, but it's still recognisably Sam because he owns the decision personally rather than hiding behind process. Never average these into one flat tone.

## The non-negotiables, every register, no exceptions

- **British / Australian English.** Colour, organise, behaviour, recognise, centre.
- **`—>` is Sam's preferred glyph at all times.** This is his one deliberate em-dash exception. Everywhere else: no em dash, ever, use a comma, full stop, semicolon, or parentheses.
- **Banned phrases:** "Here's the thing" (any variant), "It's not X, it's Y" contrasts in any form. Full `stop-slop` rules apply as the default filter, except where they'd delete a Protected Pattern in `voice-profile.md` (self-answering questions, self-aware meta-commentary, flat fragments stating a hard truth).
- **Verbosity is the single most-corrected failure.** Default to lean. Give Sam something to add to, not something to trim. If it feels complete but long, cut it again before showing him.
- **The anti-generic test is concrete vs abstract, not "is a person named."** A number, a mechanism, or a lived detail is just as concrete as a name. Run this sentence by sentence, not once over the whole piece, generic and genuine lines sit right next to each other in real drafts if you're not careful.
- **Never invent a person.** Real names only.
- **Current email:** sam@weareeight.com.au.

## 10-second sniff test before delivering

1. Does it match the actual register (see table above), not a flattened "Sam voice" average?
2. Run the anti-generic checklist per sentence.
3. Could 30-50% come out without losing anything real? If yes, cut before showing him.
4. Any banned phrase, stray em dash, or American spelling?
5. Would Sam actually send this, or does it read like an assistant trying hard to sound like him?

If any answer is off, revise before delivering.
