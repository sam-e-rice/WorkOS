# Sam Rice — GTM Pages & Outreach

Structured, candidate-facing, but the wink survives. Read `voice-profile.md` first for hard rules and the register map.

## GTM pages

**Register:** rationed chaos. Backtick emphasis on names and labels (`` `Sam` or `Danny` ``, `` `Technology Stack:` ``), emoji bookending section headers (🎓 Who Are Stile Education? 🎓), direct winking asides in brackets ("sorry design fwiends," "use the drop downs please"), one bracket-joke per document is roughly the ceiling, not five.

**Structure that recurs:** overview/hook → role TL;DR aside block → company overview → technical reality (stack, tools, what you'll build) → the real opportunity, with an honest "this isn't for everyone" callout → team dynamics → who we ARE / are NOT looking for (bulleted, blunt) → full spec download → interview process → what's next / CTA → sign-off ("Thanks for lookin' ✌️").

**"Who we're NOT looking for" is doing real work.** Bluntness here is a service, it helps the wrong candidates self-select out fast, it isn't being an arsehole. The line is: name the mismatch plainly, never mock it.

**On `—>` vs `-->`:** older GTM pages used `—>` already, before Sam formalised it as the standard everywhere. Consistent with the hard rule in `voice-profile.md`.

## Cold outreach (LinkedIn Recruiter DM)

**The template is disciplined and largely fixed.** Sam varies the content, not the shape:

1. **Title line**, its own artefact: `Role | Salary | Superlative company descriptor`, always in that order, money before any adjective.
2. **Specific flattery hook.** "Seeing you're 'open to opportunities' has got me pretty excited." Never "I came across your profile," Sam's own words: "WAYYYY over done." State exactly why you're messaging this person, not a generic opener.
3. **Self-answering rhetorical question.** "Why? 'Cos you look like a brilliant fit..." Same device as the SaaS reflective post.
4. **💎 emoji-bookended company header 💎**, same device as GTM pages.
5. **Numbers before adjectives, always.** $230m, 320 companies, 115,000 investors, before a single descriptive word.
6. **Emoji key-highlights list**, same fixed order as the ad register: money → what-you'll-do → location → culture/path.
7. **👉 link 👈 bracketing** to the full Notion GTM page. Outreach messages point to depth, they don't try to contain it.
8. **Close as a question, not a command.** "Let's jump on a call this week to discuss?" not "Book a call now." Low-pressure, frames it as the candidate's choice.

**Banned specifically here:** "I came across your profile," "excited to connect," anything that reads as a template with the name swapped in. The specificity of the hook is the entire point of this register: if it could go to anyone, it's wrong.

**What doesn't travel here from the ad register:** stretched caps, nested arrow chaos, invented words. This is confident and warm, not silly, a stranger's attention is at stake, not comic effect.
