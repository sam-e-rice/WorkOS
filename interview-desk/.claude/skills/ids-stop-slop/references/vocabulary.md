## Phrases — Openers & signposting

- "It's important to note that…" / "It's worth noting/mentioning…" / "Notably,…"
- "That being said…" / "That said…"
- "In today's fast-paced world…" (107x per GPTZero)
- "In an ever-evolving landscape…" / "In today's rapidly evolving market…"
- "In the realm of…" / "In the world of…" / "In a sea of…"
- "When it comes to…" / "At its core…" / "At the heart of…"
- "Navigating the complexities of…" / "Delve into the intricacies of…" / "Dive deep into…"
- "A deeper understanding of…" / "A nuanced take on…"
- "Let's break it down" / "Let's unpack this" / "Let's dive in" / "Let's explore"
- "Here's the kicker" / "Here's the thing" / "Here's the part most people miss" / "Here's what's interesting"
- "And honestly? That's rare." / "You're not imagining it." / "No fluff."
- "What no one else is talking about" / "What nobody tells you"
- "Sit with that for a moment"
- "Imagine a world where…" / "Picture a future in which…"
- "Whether you're a [X] or a [Y]…" (false breadth — just means "everyone")
- "I recently had the pleasure of…"
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "Based on the information provided…" / "As of my last knowledge update…"

## Phrases — Transitions & scaffolding

- "Moreover," / "Furthermore," / "Additionally," (sentence-initial; GPT-4-era hallmark)
- "Consequently," / "Accordingly," / "Hence," / "Thus," / "Thereby," / "Therein," / "Thereof"
- "In light of…" / "Given that…" / "Broadly speaking…" / "Generally speaking…"
- "In essence…" / "In brief…" / "In other words…" / "Simply put…"
- "First, we'll look at… Second, we'll examine… Finally…" (telegraphed structure)
- "The objective of the study aimed to…" (269x — GPTZero's most AI-skewed phrase)
- "Further research is needed to understand…" (235x)
- "Despite facing…" (209x)
- "Notable works include…" / "notable figures…" (120x+)
- "Aims to explore/enhance…" (50x+)
- "Shed(s) light on…" (11x+)
- "It cannot be overstated…" / "plays a vital role in…"

## Phrases — Closers & conclusion rituals

- "In conclusion…" / "In summary…" / "Overall…" / "To summarize…" / "Ultimately…"
- "At the end of the day…"
- "As we navigate [X], it's essential that we…"
- "The journey doesn't end here…" / "The road ahead…" / "The future looks bright"
- "Only time will tell" / "One thing is certain"
- "Remember, when doing X it's important to consider…"
- Generic future-narrative closers: modal + "become" + superlative + narrative/trend/chapter ("may become one of the most important narratives of the next cycle") — a prediction with no falsifiable content

## Chatbot residue (near-definitive paste artifacts)

- "I hope this helps!" / "Let me know if you'd like me to go deeper!" / "Feel free to reach out"
- "Great question!" / "You're absolutely right!" / "What a thoughtful observation!" (sycophancy)
- "Certainly!" / "Absolutely!" / "Of course!"
- "Would you like me to…" / "Is there anything else…"
- "As an AI language model…" (older models)
- "While specific details are limited in the available sources…" / "[He] maintains a low public profile" (speculative gap-filling)
- Unfilled placeholders: "[Your Name]", "[INSERT SOURCE URL]", "2025-XX-XX"

## Social-media & promotional slop

- Engagement hooks: "The catch?", "The kicker?", "Plot twist:", "The result? Devastating.", "The best part?"
- Fake-candid openers: "Honestly?", "Look,", "Real talk:", "Let's be honest —"
- Social endorsement closers: "This one is worth your time:", "A must-read:", "Don't sleep on this.", "Thank me later.", "Bookmark this."
- Lingering-attention claims: "the line I keep coming back to," "I can't stop thinking about this" (unfalsifiable unless a reason is attached)
- Recap-flattery: restating someone's own work back at them with praise before your point
- Hashtag stuffing: 6+ trailing hashtags mixing project tags with broad category tags
- Emotional flatline phrases: "What surprised me most," "I was fascinated to discover," "What struck me was"
- Narrated candor: "I want to be upfront:", "To be fully transparent:", "Two caveats I'd rather flag than let you discover later:"

## AI-vocabulary era map (from Wikipedia's observations)

- **2023 – mid-2024 (GPT-4):** Additionally, boasts, bolstered, crucial, delve, emphasizing, enduring, garner, intricate, interplay, key, landscape, meticulous, pivotal, underscore, tapestry, testament, valuable, vibrant
- **Mid-2024 – mid-2025 (GPT-4o):** align with, crucial, emphasizing, enhance, enduring, fostering, highlighting, pivotal, showcasing, underscore, vibrant
- **Mid-2025+ (GPT-5):** emphasizing, enhance, highlighting, showcasing + canned notability/media-coverage emphasis
